Went to the Khatsahlano Street Party today. I thought there would be light rain, but it ended up staying dry, which made the day even better.

This year there were lots of big brands, like Starbucks, Fresh Market, and Chapman's. It’s always fun to see what’s new each year, and that’s one of the reasons it remains one of my favorite summer events.
